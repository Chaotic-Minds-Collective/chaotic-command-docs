## Implemented application capabilities

- Public product homepage at `/` and a separate command workspace at `/command`, with OAuth returning users to the workspace.
- Discord OAuth sign-in and manageable-server listing.
- Optional shared bot and up to five customer-owned bots per account.
- Encrypted bot credentials and account-scoped bot assignments.
- Community, Gaming, and Support setup templates.
- Validated custom role, category, and text-channel structures.
- Up to 20 personal saved structures per account.
- Manual AI prompt and JSON import workflow.
- Read-only audit of supported role, category, and channel data.
- Expiring, single-use previews bound to the selected bot and relevant server permission context.
- Additive apply operations with exact confirmation, role permission review, and partial-progress reporting.
- SQLite-backed sessions, saved structures, notes, bot credentials, and assignments.

## Known limitations

- No new Discord guild creation.
- No full existing-server revamp operations such as moving, renaming, reordering, or deleting objects.
- No editing existing role permissions or private-channel permission policies.
- No moderation, tickets, onboarding, welcome messages, automatic role assignment, or long-running automations.
- No direct AI provider integration.
- No Composio adapter in the active runtime.
- Per-server operation locks are process-local. Multi-process safety is not implemented.
- Production backup automation and hot-backup recovery are not verified.
- Live Discord acceptance and browser visual/accessibility tests remain outstanding.
- The preserved Bit/React workspace has not been established as part of the standalone runtime build.
